# Elavsky Technology Consulting, LLC

Operating since 2021, **Elavsky Technology Consulting, LLC** is co-owned and operated by Frank and Shelby Elavsky. We do **engineering, design, evaluation, and R&D consulting for accessibility and interactive data science**.

> **Currently taking clients.**

## How we collaborate

We help businesses, non-profits, government orgs, and even policymakers make data more accessible for everyone, specifically focusing on accessibility for people with disabilities.

We most often work in a **freelance R&D role**: evaluating the accessibility of existing products, tools, and workflows, developing short- and long-term strategies, building out new systems alongside your engineers, training and educating your existing employees, and facilitating co-design and user research with people with disabilities.

Our industry collaborations also help advance the state of the art for the whole field, improving tools, techniques, and contributing our findings broadly.

*This mirrors the industry-facing collaboration model described on my [collab page](/collab/), where I also detail how I work with fellow researchers and students.*
